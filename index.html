Overview

The current simple, functional and responsive design and the majority of code for this site is my work.

Serving customers within the Cornish community, this website has a few rolls to play. The main is to provide heating oil delivery price quotations that can easily be converted into orders. But, this department also has a number of different segregations within itself, such as heating oil tank installation and heating oil boiler installation plus their servicing/maintenance.

Design. Build.

The design is my own, and the site was built by me, apart from some pre-existing backend code that didn't need to be amended during modernisation.

Current Version

Redesign. Redevelop. Modernise.

As with most fairly old websites, before the current version of this site, it was in a rather sorry state with the main issues being its tabled non-responsive structure, poor CTA clarity, and it's rather dated off-putting appearance.

The brief for the site was a common one when redevelopment happens, with just a few caveats.
The most obvious ones were to modernise the structure to allow for a responsive experience, a fresh new design was also needed while maintaining a similar look and familiar feel as to not be a jarringly different experience for the commonly older target audience of users. General improvements were also required when it came to the SEO performance of the site, but with the modern restructuring of the HTML it became pretty easy to produce increased SERP performance.
The site also had a rather odd aspect, as mentioned, this department has segregations within itself, and something all these sections have in common is the slight lack of grasp with technology, with the boiler department not even having a computer (This is not even a joke!?!) so low maintenance and reliance on things such as PDF price lists were a must.
